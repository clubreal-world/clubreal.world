Our community venue series continues with a special event at the Lehigh Valley Frends Meeting, a beautiful Quaker Meeting House in Bethlehem, PA.

[Shifting Harbor](https://shiftingharbor.bandcamp.com/album/cycle-of-return) - The current project of Tom Asselin, whose previous Dragon Turtle was an important part of the 00s Pennsylvania experimental + ambient/post-rock music scene. His most recent album of "sonic landscapes rotating through time" was released early March 2026. 

Encounters #1: [Sailcloth](https://sailcloth.bandcamp.com/) X [Pigeon School](https://pigeonschool.bandcamp.com/album/skylight): Encounters is a new series we are developing that pairs two artists for a collaborative, improvised set. We're excited to begin the series with a collaborative set by Sailcloth and Pigeon School, two local musicians whose work blurs boundaries across genres and sensibilities. 

[Uffdow](https://uffdow.bandcamp.com/): Oli and Friends from Philadelphia. Oli is a member of the band [Prill](https://prill.bandcamp.com/) and across several low-key releases on Bandcamp, has created a body of work melding tender yet mysterious ballads with searching vocals approaching the beauty of artists like Grouper or Maxine Funke.

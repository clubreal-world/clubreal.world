A special night of guitars, voices, coffee and pizza in Bethlehem PA. 

[The Sleeves](https://thesleeves12xu.bandcamp.com/album/the-sleeves) - The Sleeves are a guitar and voice duo of Jack Cooper and Tara Cunningham who together form one half of extraordinary UK post rock band Modern Nature. As The Sleeves, they weave delicate, complex sonic tapestries from the interplay of their voices and improvised, conversational guitar work. Their debut album on 12XU records is a deep, intimate listen that the CRW crew couldn't recommend more enthusiastically. 

[William H. Travis](https://williamhtravis.bandcamp.com/album/buckets-theres-a-hole) - What to say about William H. Travis that can adequately convey his stature in the Lehigh Valley? The bard of the valley; the scene's biggest friend and booster; writer and performer of funny, melancholy, deeply personal songs; gatherer and conducter of the fellowship of love? All of that and more. 

Past Nurture - New Choral music from ClubRealWorld's very own Charlie Biando, also of Blue Year and a resident of Bethlehem PA. 

Get there early to grab a slice of pizza from [Bub's Breads](https://www.instagram.com/bubs_breads/?hl=en) - starting at 6pm!

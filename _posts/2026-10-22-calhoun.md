[jason calhoun](https://napsounds.bandcamp.com/) -  Musician, artist, and human living and working in Philadelphia. In addition to being a member of post-folk outfit Hour, Jason has issued a steady drip of patient, textured ambient releases over the years: first as Paper Armies, then Naps, and now under his own given name.

[Molasses Hill](https://molasseshill.bandcamp.com/album/texas-sunset) - New and somewhat mysterious duo out of Easton making long-form psychedelic guitar and synthesizer music. Their first show in Bethlehem and we're excited to welcome them!

[Pigeon School](https://pigeonschool.bandcamp.com/) - CRW crew member Jason is going to be creating unstable broken and noisy ambient electronic music with the help of Lee Romich AKA [Whirlybird](https://whirlybirdd.bandcamp.com/) for this one.

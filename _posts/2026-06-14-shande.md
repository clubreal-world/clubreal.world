An almost-summer afternoon show on the back patio of Connexions Gallery in Easton, PA

[Shande](https://shande.bandcamp.com/album/hereness) - Led by songwriter Jack Braunstein, Shande makes jazz-inflected sensual folk pop Woven from diverse threads of the Philadelphia music scenes that have nurtured Braunstein’s musical sensibilities over the years, resulting sonically kaleidoscopic and explosively political music rooted in responsibility to this world. 

[Was](https://w-a-s.bandcamp.com/album/call-it-come-around) - Slow, precise, powerful guitar music from NYC.

[John Galm](https://johngalm.bandcamp.com/album/peace-in-the-valley-2) - Local legend making delicate haunting ambient folk. 

[Eilidh](https://soundcloud.com/nvm934884988) - Debut show of local electronic pop project.

ClubRealWorld Presents:

<img src="/images/deadbeat.png">

[Deadbeat Beat](https://deadbeatbeat.bandcamp.com/) - Deadbeat Beat hails from Detroit but you wouldn't be wrong wondering if they were from New Zealand, as their brand of garage rock and indie pop would fit well on the Flying Nun roster. Bonus points for a member of Tyvek who brought the house down here two summers ago.

[Eraser](https://siltbreeze.bandcamp.com/album/eraser-hideout-lp) - A very special Philadelphia combo whose unique, dissonant put tuneful post-punk is well showcased on last year's 45rpm LP from Siltbreeze Records, which is still in heavy rotation. 

[Radlands](https://radlandsmusic.bandcamp.com/album/all-alone) - Local band skirting the line between shoegaze and dark post punk. 

Blue Year - Debut show of local band entirely composed of Club Real World members. What does it sound like? Only one way to find out.

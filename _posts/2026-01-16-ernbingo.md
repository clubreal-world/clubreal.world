Join ClubRealWorld for a special bingo night to raise funds for the Lehigh Valley Emergency Response Network.

The ERN runs a rapid response line to report any ICE activity in our community (610-850-9930) and provides support to families and individuals affected by ICE arrests. Via the network, “The Lehigh Valley Emergency Response Network is a coalition of community leaders, faith leaders, nonprofit organizations, and other concerned residents who are committed to protecting immigrant communities as they face rapidly escalating discrimination, unlawful arrest, and unlawful deportation.”

Play bingo with buds ($5/bingo card), win fun prizes, and support our immigrant neighbors and friends. Bingo will be followed by an after party with <a href="https://soundcloud.com/la-er-3">DJ Chaela Chaotic</a>.

# about clubreal.world

ClubRealWorld is a collective of individuals promoting events in the Lehigh Valley, Pennsylvania.

We aim to build an inclusive DIY arts community centered around adventurous, left-of-the-dial indie music, film, and other media. 

Our events are mostly gigs with bands, DJ nights and film screenings but we're always evolving and interested in your ideas. 

While you're hanging around our corner of the world wide web, you should:

[subscribe to our newsletter](https://news.clubreal.world/),
[follow us on instagram](https://www.instagram.com/clubreal.world),
[watch videos of some of our shows on youtube](https://www.youtube.com/@ClubReal_World), and
[reach out if you are interested in working with us!](/contact)

See you in the real world

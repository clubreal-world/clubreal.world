[Chime School](https://chimeschool.bandcamp.com/album/the-boy-who-ran-the-paisley-hotel) - Led by Bay Area songwriter/multi-instrumentalist Andy Pastalaniec, Chime School crafts shimmering, hook-laden jangle pop inspired by the Byrds, Sarah Records, Creation Records, and the golden era of indie pop. 

[Trinket](https://itstrinket.bandcamp.com/album/new-hobby) - Hailing from Brooklyn, NY and fronted by Madison Kate (also of Betty Brite and Lightheaded), Trinket blends noisy guitars with luminous melodies, landing somewhere between dream pop, indie rock, and classic jangle. 

[Big Boned](https://bigboned-4ever.bandcamp.com/track/no-end-in-sight) - Big Boned is the Bethlehem-based project of songwriter Leah Tagliarino who pairs folky, emo-leaning confessionals with an infectious sense of melody. 

[Me at the Zoo](https://vacantstarerecords.bandcamp.com/album/vol-1) - Me at the Zoo plays fuzzed-out power pop with big hooks and loud guitars.

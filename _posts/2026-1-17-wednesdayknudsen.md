Dinner and drones at Nawab Indian Restaurant in Bethlehem, PA, featuring:

[Wednesday Knudsen](https://wednesdayknudsen.bandcamp.com/album/atrium-2) - Enjoy minimalist organic tone-scapes from this veteran of the Northeast experimental scene and member of groups such as Pigeons, Stella Kola and more. Her new album Atrium is out now on Feeding Tube Records/Spinster Sounds. 

[Elaheh Roya](https://elahehroya.bandcamp.com/album/ancestral-calling) - Bathe in the healing drones and chants of Elaheh, a Lehigh Valley based Iranian immigrant, singer and writer- Using her voice is her ancestral calling and an act towards collective liberation.  

[Pigeon School](https://pigeonschool.bandcamp.com/album/skylight) - Lo-fi ambience from the valley.
